Serpentine

The twisty way there and back.

Pick a start and how far you want to ride. Serpentine finds a loop on curvy, quiet roads, then hands it to Apple Maps for turn-by-turn voice guidance.

Join the beta In TestFlight now · coming to the App Store · iPhone and iPad
Loops

Curves first, minutes second.

Navigation apps take the fastest road. Serpentine prefers the ones worth riding: plenty of bends, moderate speed limits, little traffic, no freeway if it can help it.

Set the distance and how twisty you want it. Don't like the result? Tap for another.

Out and back

Different roads on the way home.

Pick a direction and Serpentine rides you out to a turnaround, then brings you back on other roads wherever the map allows. It won't just retrace your steps.

Electric

Charging stops for the Zero SR/S.

Turn on charging and Serpentine plans stops at public J1772 and Tesla destination chargers along the route. It estimates your charge on arrival and how long you'll wait.

The estimates err on the cautious side. A stop you didn't need beats a long walk.

Ride

Voice guidance you already trust.

One tap opens the ride in Apple Maps with stops along the planned roads, so Siri follows Serpentine's route rather than inventing its own. Need it in another app? Export a GPX file.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, ads or analytics. Your start point is used to plan the ride and isn't kept. Privacy policy

Pay once

No subscription. The routing runs on a server we own, so there's no per-ride bill to pass on to you.

Western US

Currently plans rides in the western United States. Requires iOS 18.4 or later on iPhone or iPad.
