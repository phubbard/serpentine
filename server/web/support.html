Serpentine — Support

Serpentine plans motorcycle rides on curvy, quiet roads — loops and out-and-backs — and hands them to Apple Maps for voice guidance. For the Zero SR/S it also plans charging stops.

Contact

Email pfh@phfactor.net with questions, bug reports or a ride that came out wrong (a screenshot and where you started helps).

Can I plan by time instead of distance?

Yes — switch the plan screen from Distance to Time and say how long you have. Serpentine aims to bring you back under that time, and counts any charging stop against it.

Where it works

The western United States. Starting points outside that area can't be planned yet.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.4 or later, and an internet connection to plan. Navigation uses the Apple Maps app.

Why does Apple Maps show several stops?

Apple Maps plans its own route between points, so Serpentine gives it stops along the planned roads to keep it on them. Tap to continue after each one.

How accurate are the charging estimates?

Deliberately cautious: Serpentine would rather suggest a stop you didn't need than strand you. Stops are at public Level 2 J1772 and Tesla destination chargers, charging at the bike's 6.6 kW. Charger information comes from the U.S. Department of Energy and can be out of date; check a charger before you depend on it.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Details in the privacy policy.
